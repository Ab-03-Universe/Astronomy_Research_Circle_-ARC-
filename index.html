Exploring the universe.

Astronomy Research Circle (ARC) is a student-driven initiative dedicated to astronomy, space science, scientific education, observation and research.

Discover ARC Explore Programmes
01 / ABOUT ARC

Curiosity is where science begins.

ARC brings together students, educators, researchers and astronomy enthusiasts to explore the universe through observation, experimentation and scientific discussion.

Astronomy Research Circle is an initiative created to make astronomy and space science accessible beyond the traditional classroom.

We believe that scientific learning becomes meaningful when students can observe, experiment, question and communicate their understanding.

ARC therefore combines astronomy education, observational activities, scientific projects, expert interactions and STEM outreach.

“Look up. Ask questions. Understand what you see.”

04 / ISRO SPACE TUTOR

Inspiring the next generation.

Proposed ISRO Space Tutor Initiative

ARC proposes to contribute to space-science outreach by creating accessible educational opportunities for students to understand India's space programme, astronomy and space technology.

Through lectures, demonstrations, observations, expert interactions and student projects, ARC aims to help young learners discover the science behind India's exploration of space.

05 / VISION

A community looking upward.

ARC envisions a community where a student can look at the night sky and not simply see stars, but begin to ask scientific questions about them.

We want to build bridges between curiosity, education, observation, research and the wider scientific community.
